# Card game ideas (backlog)

Back to [[Home]] · see [[Card games]] for the shared deck and the gambling rule.

Giovanni's list of card games to consider, ranked for Game Night (parties of friends on Roblox, bots fill seats, all ages). Built so far: Color Clash (the Crazy Eights family), [[Speed]], [[Spoons]], [[War]], [[Go Fish]], and all of Tier 1: [[I Doubt It]], [[President]], [[Slap Stack]], [[Hearts]], [[Spades]], [[Thirty-One]], [[Palace]]. Names with crude words get clean names in the lobby (set in `GameCatalog`).

## Tier 1: built (best fit for Roblox parties)
| Game | Players | Category | Why | Lobby name |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| Cheat | 3-8 | Social | Bluffing is great with friends; bots can bluff with simple odds | "I Doubt It" |
| President | 3-8 | Climbing | Easy, funny ranks each round | "President" |
| Egyptian Ratscrew | 2-6 | Slap / speed | Real-time slapping like Speed | "Slap Stack" |
| Hearts | 4 | Trick-taking | The classic online card game; no partners needed | "Hearts" |
| Spades | 4 (2 teams) | Trick-taking | Huge online player base; party members can team up | "Spades" |
| Blitz / Thirty-One | 2-8 | Draw and discard | Quick rounds; play with lives (hearts), never chips | "Thirty-One" |
| Palace | 2-6 | Shedding | Light and popular with teens | "Palace" |

## Tier 2: good later
Old Maid, Snap / Slap Jack, Gin Rummy, Oh Hell, Crazy Eights (Color Clash covers it; could be a 52-card mode), Knock Out Whist, Fan Tan (Sevens), Euchre, Nertz.

## Tier 3: niche or complex, skip for now
Bridge, Skat, Piquet, Pinochle, Zetema, Mao, Eleusis, Bezique, Jass.

## Also on Giovanni's list
- Rummy and Contract Rummy, Scopa / Escoba / Cassino, GOPS, Ranter-Go-Round (lives, not chips), 500, Briscola: fine to build, not ranked yet.

## Not allowed
- Poker, blackjack and any casino game: Roblox bans playable simulated gambling. See [[Card games]].

# Property Party

Back to [[Home]].

A property-trading board game for 2-8 seats (buy streets, charge rent, build, trade, bankrupt everyone else), with our own name and board: Party Town. The display name is set only in `shared/GameCatalog.luau`; the internal id and module are `Estates` so a rename never touches code.

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/Estates.luau`. The board (40 spaces: 22 streets in 8 color groups, 4 stations, 2 utilities, 3 Lucky Break and 3 Town Mail card spaces, 2 taxes, 4 corners) and both card decks are data at the top of the file. A themed board is a content swap: new names, colors and card text.
- Turn: roll two dice and move. Unowned deed: buy it, or it goes to auction. Owned deed: pay rent (doubled for a full color set with no buildings; then houses, hotel; stations by how many are owned; utilities 4x or 10x the dice). Passing Start pays $200. Doubles roll again; a third double is Jail.
- Jail: pay $50 or use a Get Out of Jail card before rolling, or roll for doubles. The third failed try pays $50 and moves. Rent is still collected from Jail.
- Building: a full color set with no mortgages, built and sold evenly. The bank has 32 houses and 12 hotels. Selling pays half. Selling a hotel when the bank is short of houses sells the whole set down to the highest even level the bank can supply (`Estates.hotelSale`). Mortgaging pays half the price; paying it off costs that plus 10%. Taking over a mortgaged deed (trade or bankruptcy) costs the 10% fee straight away (`Estates.mortgageFee`, `feesFor`). No mortgaging while the color has buildings. House rule `quickHotels`: a hotel comes after 3 houses (`state.hotelAt`).
- Auctions: live. Everyone still playing (the one who declined too) can bid at once; every bid restarts an 8-second countdown (`Estates.getTimer`, 5 s in fast mode), and when it runs out the top bid pays (`onTimeout`). Passing drops you out; if nobody is left to challenge the leader it ends at once. No bids leaves the deed with the bank. Active seats during an auction are everyone in it except the leader.
- Debt: owing more cash than you have puts the turn in a `debt` phase: sell, mortgage or trade, then pay, or go bankrupt. If everything you own can't cover it, you're bankrupt at once. Bankrupt to a player: they get your cash, deeds (still mortgaged, 10% fee each) and Jail cards. To the bank: every deed goes up for auction one after another (`state.auctionQueue`). Buildings are sold to the bank first. You can't trade away more than you could raise while in debt (`canTradeOut`).
- Trades: on your own turn (also while in debt), up to 3 offers a turn. Off turn, every other player may send one offer per turn while the turn player is between actions (`getInterruptSeats`, `view.canOffer`); the turn player answers it. Deeds, cash and Get Out of Jail cards, both ways. Streets in a color with buildings can't be traded. Deals between people that give away more than half of a player's worth for less than a quarter back are refused (no kingmaking). The other player accepts, declines or sends back a counter-offer (`counter`, up to 2 per trade), which the first player then answers.
- Cards: 16 in each deck, like the classic decks, including "pay each player $50" and "collect $10 from each player" and an advance to Sushi Street.
- Length: by default the game ends after `Estates.roundsFor(seats)` rounds (30 rounds for 2 players, 25 for 4, 17 for 6, 15 for 8) and the highest net worth wins (cash + deed prices, half for mortgaged + building costs). House rule `length`: Short (70%), Normal, Long (150%), Until one is left (`maxRounds = 0`, `view.endless`) or First bankruptcy ends it (richest wins). Bot-only test games take about 250 moves.
- House rules (Party Host): `fast` (2 random deeds each, never a whole set; 5 s auctions; 70% of the rounds; hotels after 3 houses), `auctions` (off: a declined deed stays unsold), `jackpot` (bank payments pile up in `state.pot`, paid out on Rest Stop), `doubleStart` ($400 for landing right on Start), `quickHotels`, `length`, `firstLap`.
- Simplified from the classic rules: utility card uses a fresh roll.
- Table: `src/client/Renderers/Estates.luau`, laid out like the big online versions. 11x11 board drawn in code (owner outline in their seat color, houses as squares, HOTEL, MORTGAGED), tokens that hop space by space, dice, jackpot, the last card and a news feed in the middle. Right side: every player's cash (counts up/down with +$/-$ pop-ups; a single matching payment flies as bills from payer to owner) and deeds as color chips (tap one for its card, tap a player to trade), then the main button (Roll / End turn, which nudges after a while / Pay) with Manage and Trade.
- Pop-ups (on the ScreenGui, centered left of the side panel, so they sit over the table in Table view): deed card when anyone lands on a free deed (Buy / Auction for the lander), the live auction (top bid, countdown bar with Going once / twice, who's still in, +1 / +10 / +100 / Pass (out), and Raise cash, which opens Manage without leaving the auction), Jail choices, a trade offer (Accept / Counter / Decline), the Trade screen (two columns, cash steppers, Jail cards, tap deeds on the board to add them), the Manage screen (every set with − / + houses and Mortgage / Pay off) and any deed card you tap. Every roll tumbles big in the middle and drawn cards flip over; the next pop-up waits until the dice and token have settled (`busyUntil`).
- More buttons: off-turn Trade (one offer a turn), "+30 s" (once per turn, `MatchRequest` action `extend`, `Config.ExtendSeconds`), Log (the full game log in a pop-up; the news feed keeps the last 8), and an Auto end toggle that ends your turn by itself once nothing is left to do. Trade steppers have +500 and All. The round label shows "Round N" when the game has no round limit.
- Big moments (once the token has arrived): a BANKRUPT call-out with a thud and the player's row shaking, a hotel call-out with applause, a JACKPOT call-out with confetti, and "+$200 Start" floating off the token. The 2D token hops to where the dice landed and stops there before a card or Go to Jail moves it on (`players[s].landed = { pos, n }`, set in `moveBy`). Auctions take a typed bid (Enter sends it; too low or too high says why), and the trade screen's cash amount is a box you can type into.
- Table view: deed spaces on the 3D board have `TapId` attributes and `CanQuery`; `Table3D/Controller` raycasts taps on watch-only scenes and MatchView passes the id to `renderer.tapTable`, which opens the deed card.
- 3D table: tokens hop space by space after a roll (`Boards/Estates.luau`, tokens live outside the redraw); cards and Jail glide in one arc.
- Bot: keeps a cash reserve (easy and medium $50, hard $30; a $200 reserve made hard bots lose 16-44 to easy ones in simulations because they bought and built less). Buys when it can keep the reserve; medium/hard buy anything early (under 40% of deeds owned) and always buy a street that finishes or blocks a set. Medium/hard build down to half their reserve. Bids up to the deed's value to it (more if it finishes a set). Builds on full sets with spare cash (easy stops at 3 houses). Medium/hard try to finish a set by trade, once per deal: a swap first (one of their streets that finishes a set for the other player, the price gap squared up in cash), otherwise a cash premium (twice the price of the street plus a house's cost per street of the set; hard 1.2x, easy 0.8x). They mortgage a loose deed to afford a street that finishes a set. Accepts trades that raise its holdings value; hands someone a new set only for a set back or for a premium that covers the income it gives away (`tradeIncome`). Close deals get a counter-offer instead of a no. It also turns down giveaways (it gets more than 1.5x what it gives plus $100), so nobody can pour money into a bot. In Jail it pays early and stays in late. To raise cash it mortgages loose deeds first, then sells houses, then mortgages sets.
- Coins: Property Coins (`coin = "property"`) with their own shop tab in `shared/ShopCatalog.luau`.
- Tests: `tests/Estates.spec.luau` (rent, buying, auctions, building limits, mortgages, Jail, debt, bankruptcy, trades, cards, the round limit, and bot-only games at every level that check houses and hotels are never lost and cash never goes negative).
- Not yet: 3D board and token models (`Assets.Estates`), dice/token cosmetics drawn in game, a starting-cash house rule.

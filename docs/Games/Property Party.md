# Property Party

Back to [[Home]].

A property-trading board game for 2-8 seats (buy streets, charge rent, build, trade, bankrupt everyone else), with our own name and board: Party Town. The display name is set only in `shared/GameCatalog.luau`; the internal id and module are `Estates` so a rename never touches code.

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/Estates.luau`. The board (40 spaces: 22 streets in 8 color groups, 4 stations, 2 utilities, 3 Lucky Break and 3 Town Mail card spaces, 2 taxes, 4 corners) and both card decks are data at the top of the file. A themed board is a content swap: new names, colors and card text.
- Turn: roll two dice and move. Unowned deed: buy it, or it goes to auction. Owned deed: pay rent (doubled for a full color set with no buildings; then houses, hotel; stations by how many are owned; utilities 4x or 10x the dice). Passing Start pays $200. Doubles roll again; a third double is Jail.
- Jail: pay $50 or use a Get Out of Jail card before rolling, or roll for doubles. The third failed try pays $50 and moves. Rent is still collected from Jail.
- Building: a full color set with no mortgages, built and sold evenly. The bank has 32 houses and 12 hotels. Selling pays half. Mortgaging pays half the price; paying it off costs that plus 10%. No mortgaging while the color has buildings.
- Auctions: everyone still playing bids in turn, starting after the player who declined. Bid or pass; the last bidder standing pays. No bids leaves the deed with the bank.
- Debt: owing more cash than you have puts the turn in a `debt` phase: sell, mortgage or trade, then pay, or go bankrupt. If everything you own can't cover it, you're bankrupt at once. Bankrupt to a player: they get your cash, deeds (still mortgaged) and Jail cards. To the bank: deeds go back up for sale. Buildings are sold to the bank first.
- Trades: on your own turn (also while in debt), up to 3 offers a turn. Deeds, cash and Get Out of Jail cards, both ways. Streets in a color with buildings can't be traded. The other player answers (the turn waits on them, with the turn timer).
- Speed: card money always comes from or goes to the bank (no "pay each player" cards). The game ends after `Estates.roundsFor(seats)` rounds (30 rounds for 2 players, 25 for 4, 17 for 6, 15 for 8) and the highest net worth wins (cash + deed prices, half for mortgaged + building costs). Bot-only test games take about 250 moves.
- Simplified from the classic rules: trades only on your turn, no 10% fee for taking over a mortgaged deed, utility card uses a fresh roll, no Free Parking money.
- Table: `src/client/Renderers/Estates.luau`. 11x11 board drawn in code (owner outline in their seat color, houses as squares, HOTEL, MORTGAGED, round tokens), dice and the last card in the middle with a news feed. Right side: what you can do now (roll, buy, bid, pay, accept a trade) and the deed you tapped, with Build / Sell / Mortgage / Pay off when it's yours. The Trade window picks a player, deeds on both sides, cash and Jail cards.
- Bot: keeps a cash reserve (easy $50, medium $150, hard $200). Buys when it can keep the reserve, and medium/hard always buy a street that finishes or blocks a set. Bids up to the deed's value to it (more if it finishes a set). Builds on full sets with spare cash (easy stops at 3 houses). Medium/hard offer cash (1.5x / 1.8x the price) for the one street that finishes their set, once per deal. Accepts trades that raise its holdings value and never hands someone a new set without getting one. It also turns down giveaways (it gets more than 1.5x what it gives plus $100), so nobody can pour money into a bot. In Jail it pays early and stays in late. To raise cash it mortgages loose deeds first, then sells houses, then mortgages sets.
- Coins: Property Coins (`coin = "property"`) with their own shop tab in `shared/ShopCatalog.luau`.
- Tests: `tests/Estates.spec.luau` (rent, buying, auctions, building limits, mortgages, Jail, debt, bankruptcy, trades, cards, the round limit, and bot-only games at every level that check houses and hotels are never lost and cash never goes negative).
- Not yet: 3D board and token models (`Assets.Estates`), dice/token cosmetics drawn in game, house-rule toggles (Free Parking money, no auctions, starting cash) for Party Host, a token move animation.

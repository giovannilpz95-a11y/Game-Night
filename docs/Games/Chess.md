# Chess

Back to [[Home]].

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/Chess.luau`. FIDE movement rules: castling, en passant, promotion (defaults to queen; the field must be q/r/b/n or missing), checkmate, stalemate. Draws are automatic, chess.com style: threefold repetition, the 50-move rule, insufficient material (not in King of the Hill, and only bare kings in Three-check, like Lichess).
- Board: `src/client/Renderers/Chess.luau`. Tap a piece, then a highlighted square. Includes a promotion picker, last-move and check highlights, captured pieces, and a move list in standard notation.
- Table: taken pieces stand beside the 3D board on the taker's right, with the material lead ("+3") over the row. The 2D view shows taken pieces as symbols plus the lead. The lead comes from `view.lead` (`Chess.materialLead`: material on the board, so promotions count). The frame has a-h / 1-8 coordinates (`Square.coordinates`), and the promotion picker shows the pieces.
- Tests: `tests/Chess.spec.luau`. Perft counts from 5 standard positions catch move-generation bugs.
- Squares are 0..63 (a1 = 0). Pieces are numbers, positive for White (seat 1) and negative for Black (seat 2). Over the network a move is `{ from = "e2", to = "e4", promotion = "q"? }`.
- Bot: negamax with alpha-beta and principal variation search, late move reductions, a transposition table (Zobrist keys), killer moves, MVV-LVA ordering, quiescence on captures, a check extension, iterative deepening. Legality is checked lazily (pseudo-legal moves, skipped if they leave the king in check). Evaluation: material, piece-square tables, an endgame king table and a mop-up term (drive the lone king to the edge, bring your own king close), so K+Q v K and K+R v K get mated (tests check it).
  - Easy: 1 ply, picks any move within 1.2 pawns of the best, and 15% of the time any move that doesn't lose at once (a beginner's slip).
  - Medium: 3 ply, 0.25 s.
  - Hard: up to 12 ply in 0.45 s; in practice about 5 ply in the opening and 4 in a busy middlegame. (The search blocks the whole server while it runs, so budgets stay well under a second.)
  - Repetitions: positions from the game so far (since the last capture or pawn move) and from the line being searched score as a draw inside the search.
  - Think time (`getBotDelay`): about 1-3 s on top of the search, longer with more legal moves.
- Bot search runs on the server and doesn't yield. If many bot games run at once, move it to an Actor (Parallel Luau).
- Models: `ReplicatedStorage.Assets.Chess.<Piece>_<White|Black>` (see `client/UI/PieceView.luau`).
- Online extras (like chess.com and Lichess):
  - Draw offers, takebacks and aborts go through `src/shared/Offers.luau`: `{ type = "offer", kind = "draw" | "takeback" }`, then `{ type = "answer", accept }` from the other seat, or `{ type = "abort" }` before either side's first move (no result, nothing paid). You can ask on your turn or right after your move (the game lists you in `getInterruptSeats` then). While an offer is open only the answerer is active, so the AFK clock and bots answer it (bots accept takebacks, and draws when they aren't ahead). A human whose clock runs out always turns the offer down (`Match.playForSeat`). A takeback on your turn undoes two plies; asked on the opponent's turn it undoes just your last move (3 per player). A declined draw can't be offered again for 10 plies. `getTurnKey` is the ply count, so offers don't restart the turn clock. Offers come to the client as `view.menuActions` (the ⋯ menu) and `view.ask` (the prompt over the board, drawn by `MatchView`).
  - Premoves: `Chess.premoves(fen, seat)` is piece geometry only, like Lichess's board: sliders ignore blockers, pawns may premove either diagonal, castling can be queued while king and rook are home with none of your pieces between. The 2D and 3D boards queue one and send it when the turn comes if it's legal.
  - Sounds for captures, checks, castling and promotions (`chessCapture`, `chessCheck`, `chessCastle`, `chessPromote` in `Sounds.luau`), keyed off the move's notation.
  - Flip button, left/right arrow keys to step through moves (2D board only), and PGN: a selectable box with the game as PGN (`Chess.pgn`) to paste into Lichess or chess.com.
  - Game Review (`src/shared/ChessReview.luau`): the opening book is every position on the lichess-org/chess-openings lines (CC0, 3,177 names, built by `tools/build-chess-openings.py` into `src/shared/Games/Data/ChessOpening*.luau`), matched by position so transpositions count. Book and forced moves get no "better was" arrow. Accuracy uses Lichess's aggregation (volatility-weighted mean + harmonic mean, evals capped at 10 pawns), and the eval bar shows a number ("+1.2", "M3"). The review searches 6 ply / 12,000 nodes per position in a coroutine that yields every ~12 ms, so it never hitches.
  - Drag to move on the 2D board, dots for quiet moves and rings for captures, coordinates on the edge squares.
  - Move list: every move is a button; `view.past[i]` is the FEN after move i, `view.startFen` the start. `<`, `>` and Live step through.
- Right-click marks on the 2D board: right-click a square for a ring, right-drag for an arrow; any left click clears them. Only you see them.
- Clocks: the `timeControl` house rule (Casual, 1+0, 3+2, 5+0, 10+0) turns on real chess clocks from `shared/GameClock`. The module sets `clock = true`, so `Match.submitMove` stamps each move with the server time (`move.at`) and the game takes the time used off the mover's bank, plus the increment. `getTimer` hands the server the side to move's bank as a phase clock (a new id each ply), and `onTimeout` flags them; a flag against a side that can't mate is a draw. As on Lichess, the clocks start once both sides have moved (the normal turn timer runs before that, and Abort is open). Offers settle the running clock; an offer not answered within `GameClock.OFFER_WAIT` seconds is declined. `view.clock` carries both banks, and `client/UI/ClockPills` counts the running one down.

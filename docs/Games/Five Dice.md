# Five Dice

Back to [[Home]] · see [[Tables]].

1-6 seats. The five-dice scoring game (the public-domain Yacht family), with our own name and box names ("Big Five" for five of a kind). The display name is set in `GameCatalog`.

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/FiveDice.luau`. 13 boxes (Ones to Sixes, 3/4 of a kind, Full house 25, Small straight 30, Large straight 40, Big Five 50, Chance). Upper total 63+ pays a 35 bonus. Each extra Big Five after a 50 in the Big Five box pays +100 and is a joker (official placement: its upper box first, then any lower box in full, then an upper box for 0).
- Moves: `roll` (with an optional `held` mask of 5 booleans), `hold` (just shows your keeps to the room; the next roll uses them), `score` (a box id). Three rolls a turn; scoring ends the turn.
- House rules: **Everyone rolls at once** (`together`: every seat is active, each round ends when all have scored, so 6-player games take as long as 1-player ones), **Score card** (`card = "quick"`: the 7 lower boxes only, no upper bonus), **Big Five bonus** (`bigBonus`: `true` = official forced joker, `"free"` = the joker may go in any open box and lower boxes still score in full (`state.jokerFree`), `false` = no +100 and no joker).
- View: everything is public (dice, holds, cards). The seat that is rolling also gets `preview[box]` (what this roll scores in each box it may use) and `joker`.
- AFK: `turnSeconds = 30`, once per turn: `FiveDice.getTurnKey` (round + who is rolling) tells `MatchService` that rolls and holds are the same turn, so they don't restart the clock. When it runs out `chooseAfkMove` plays the rest of your turn with the medium bot minus its noise (bot level `"afk"`), never the table's hard planner.
- Bots: easy keeps its most common face and takes the biggest raw score (with noise). Medium looks one roll ahead with noisy judgment. Hard plans every roll left: an expected-value search over the 252 ways five dice can land (`OUTCOMES`, `KEEPS` are built once), valuing each box as its points minus what that box is usually worth later (`BASELINE`), plus what the box does to the chance of the +35 upper bonus (`bonusChance`: each open upper box is modelled as about three of its face with a one-die spread, so the bot sees being 6 ahead or 4 behind across the whole section, not only when 63 is reached). Solo averages over 100 games: medium about 205, hard about 244 with the bonus in about 64% of games (was 234 and 27% before the bonus model). Slowest hard move in the audit: about 0.3-0.45 s under luau-web. Hard beat easy 36-4 heads up.
- Table (2D): `src/client/Renderers/FiveDice.luau`. Felt tray with the dice of whoever is rolling (tap to keep: the die drops onto a gold KEEP line), Roll and Score, and the score card with a column per player and faded previews in your column. Tap a box to pick it, then Score (or tap it again). Scoring 0 when another box would score asks once ("0 points in Chance. Sure?"). The card has a Lower total row. The joker hint says what the server allows ("It must go in your Fours", "Any open lower box scores in full"). In Table view the card is docked on the right and the dice and buttons move to its top.
- Table (3D): `src/server/Tables/Boards/FiveDice.luau`. Dice tray in the middle, held dice following the curve of the rim on the roller's side, the cup by the roller while they have rolls left (tap it to roll; the felt isn't tappable, so a near miss on a die never rerolls), a score pad at each seat with the last box floating over it.
- Stats: `bigFives` (once a turn, on the roll that first lands five of a kind), `upperBonus`; best score. Badge `fivedice_big` (10 Big Fives).
- Tests: `tests/FiveDice.spec.luau`.
- Holding Roll shakes the cup (the loose dice rattle); letting go rolls them.

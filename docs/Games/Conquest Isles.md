# Conquest Isles

Back to [[Home]].

A world-conquest strategy game for 2-6 seats on our own map, the Shattered Isles. The display name is set only in `shared/GameCatalog.luau`; the internal id and module are `Conquest`, so a rename never touches code.

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/Conquest.luau`. The map is data at the top of the file: 6 regions (Frostreach +1, Sunspire Sands +2, Heartwood +2, Ironcoast +1, Coral Keys +1, Emberfall +1), 24 territories with x/y positions, and the borders (two are sea lanes). A new map is a content swap.
- Setup: territories are dealt at random and every seat gets the same number of armies (30 for 2 players down to 15 for 6), spread at random. No placement phase.
- Turn: `reinforce` (income is territories / 3, at least 3, plus whole-region bonuses, plus 2 "rally" armies while you hold fewer territories than the average player still in; place with `place`), then `attack` (`attack` with `blitz` to roll until it's decided), `occupy` after a conquest when there's a choice of how many to move in, then `endTurn` or one `fortify` move through your own land (which also ends the turn).
- Conquest cap: at most `Conquest.conquestLimit(round)` territories taken per turn (1 in rounds 1-2, 2 in rounds 3-5, then 3). Added after the second playtest, where a medium bot held 14 of 24 lands by round 3.
- Dice: attacker up to 3 (one less than the armies there), defender up to 2, highest pairs compared, ties to the defender.
- Cards: 8 Swords, 8 Shields, 8 Crowns, 2 Wilds. One card for a turn with a conquest. Fixed set values: 3 Swords 3, 3 Shields 4, 3 Crowns 5, one of each 6. Holding 5+ forces a trade before placing. Knocking a player out takes their cards; holding more than 5 after that drops you back into `reinforce` to trade and place before attacking on.
- Speed: one clock per turn (`Conquest.TURN_SECONDS` = 90, via `getTimer`). When it runs out, the turn finishes itself: forced trades, leftover armies to the most threatened border, end turn. The game is scored after `Conquest.roundsFor(seats)` rounds (20 for 2 players, 16, 14, 11, 9 for 6): most territories wins, then most armies; a full tie is a draw.
- Table: `src/client/Renderers/Conquest.luau`. The map is drawn in code: a round land patch in the region's color under each territory (regions never overlap, nothing touches the frame edge), each region's name and bonus in open sea next to it (`labelX`/`labelY` in the map data), border lines, round markers in the owner's seat color with the army count, ringed in the region color. Tap your territory to select it: red rings are attack targets, green rings are where you can move armies. The side panel has the actions (place +1 / +3 / All, Roll, Blitz, move counter, End turn), your cards (pick 3 or trade the best set), the last battle's dice, who holds each region, and the news feed.
- Bot: easy places at random and attacks at even odds or better 60% of the time. Medium picks a focus region (the one it holds most of), stacks one border next to it, attacks at 1.5x odds or better, takes one fair fight a turn to earn a card, and moves stuck armies to the most threatened border. Hard attacks at 1.25x, guards regions it holds, goes after other players' whole regions, and hunts players with 1-2 territories for their cards. Bots always blitz.
- Coins: Conquest Coins (`coin = "conquest"`) with their own shop tab in `shared/ShopCatalog.luau`. Rewards come from `MatchService.onMatchFinished` like every game.
- Tests: `tests/Conquest.spec.luau` (map is connected, setup, income, card sets, dice, occupy, blitz, fortify paths, forced trades, knock-outs, round limit, the turn clock, hidden hands, and bot-only games at every level that check no army or card is lost). Bot-only games take 190-490 moves. Balance (after the first Studio playtest, where one medium bot snowballed to 83 armies by round 9): low bonuses and set values plus the rally keep it close. With the conquest cap, 20-game bot simulations reach the round limit in all 3-6 seat games at every level except a few 3-seat ones (2 of 20), and in 13-17 of 20 two-seat games. The leader holds about 6-12 lands after round 3 (an even share).
- Not yet: 3D table and army models (`Assets.Conquest`), dice and army cosmetics drawn in game, more maps, house rules for Party Host (secret missions, fog of war, no round limit).

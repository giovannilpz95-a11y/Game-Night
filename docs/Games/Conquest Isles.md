# Conquest Isles

Back to [[Home]].

A world-conquest strategy game for 2-6 seats on our own maps, the Shattered Isles and the Sunken Crown. The display name is set only in `shared/GameCatalog.luau`; the internal id and module are `Conquest`, so a rename never touches code.

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/Conquest.luau`. Maps are data in `Conquest.maps` (regions with bonus, color and label spot, territories with x/y, borders with `sea` lanes, starting armies and rounds by seat count, mission counts and region sets, turn-order armies). A new map is a content swap.
  - `isles`, The Shattered Isles: 6 regions (Frostreach +1, Sunspire Sands +2, Heartwood +2, Ironcoast +1, Coral Keys +1, Emberfall +1), 24 territories, two sea lanes.
  - `crown`, The Sunken Crown: 7 regions round the Crownlands (+3, 6 lands, borders every other region): Northwatch +1, Gale Peaks +1, Sunreach +2, Mistfen +1, Tidecaller Reef +1, Driftwood Isles +1; 31 territories on a staggered grid, 49 borders (12 between regions, so every small region has 3 ways in), sea lanes out to the isles. Mistfen's and Crownlands' names float on signs over the 3D board (`float`), the sea between rows being too narrow to lie on.
  - House rule `map`: Auto (Isles for 2-4 players, Crown for 5-6; `Conquest.pickMap`) / Shattered Isles / Sunken Crown. `state.mapId` and `view.mapId`/`mapName`; states without one are the Isles.
  - One module serves every match, so every entry point (getView, applyMove, ...) switches to its match's map with `Conquest.useMap` and puts the previous one back (wrappers at the bottom of the file); the helpers (`Conquest.territories`, `neighbors`, `regionOwner`, ...) read the current map. The client calls `useMap(view.mapId)` on each update and rebuilds its map drawing when the id changes; the 3D board builds its islands on the first update.
- Setup: territories are dealt at random and every seat gets the same number of armies (Isles 30 for 2 players down to 15 for 6, Crown 38 down to 20), spread at random. No placement phase. Turn-order armies (`seatBonus`, logged at the start): with 6 players the first seat gets +4 and the second +2 on both maps, and the second seat gets +4 in a 2-player Crown game. Medium-bot wins per 150 by seat, without and with: Isles 6 seats 16/24/25/24/26/35 and 30/20/26/21/16/35; Crown 2 seats 101/49 and 74/76; Crown 6 seats 13/23/18/28/32/38 and 25/28/27/25/22/26. Crown 3 and 5 seats were fair without (30/36/34 per 100; 30/30/31/22/39 per 150).
- Turn: `reinforce` (income is territories / 3, at least 3, plus whole-region bonuses, plus 2 "rally" armies while you hold at least a whole territory fewer than the average player still in; place with `place`; `view.income` lists the parts). Still holding a set after placing everything at the start of a turn, the turn waits in `reinforce` (`view.holdForTrade`) for a `trade` or `startAttack`, then `attack` (`attack` with `blitz` to roll until it's decided), `occupy` after a conquest when there's a choice of how many to move in, then `endTurn` or one `fortify` move through your own land (which also ends the turn).
- Conquest cap (house rule Slow start, on by default): at most `Conquest.conquestLimit(round)` territories taken per turn (1 in rounds 1-2, 2 in rounds 3-5, then 3). Added after the second playtest, where a medium bot held 14 of 24 lands by round 3. With 3+ seats round 1 is for placing only (`isPeaceRound`): otherwise everyone could hit the first seat before its second turn.
- Dice: attacker up to 3 (one less than the armies there; `attack.dice` picks fewer for a single roll), defender up to 2, highest pairs compared, ties to the defender. A blitz can stop early (`attack.stopAt`: stop once the attacking land is down to that many).
- Cards: 8 Swords, 8 Shields, 8 Crowns, 2 Wilds; each non-wild card shows a territory (all 24 on the Isles, 24 of 31 on the Crown) (deck entries are `{ kind, land }`, hands stay a list of kinds with `state.handLands` beside them). Trading a card whose territory you hold puts 2 armies there (one +2 per trade). One card for a turn with a conquest. Fixed set values: 3 Swords 3, 3 Shields 4, 3 Crowns 5, one of each 6. Holding 5+ forces a trade before placing. Knocking a player out takes their cards; holding more than 5 after that drops you back into `reinforce` (`knockoutTrade`) to trade until you hold 4 or fewer, place, and attack on.
- Speed: one clock per turn (`Conquest.TURN_SECONDS` = 90, via `getTimer`; +30 s in round 1 for the how-to card; house rule 60 s to 5 min). When it runs out, the turn finishes itself: forced trades, leftover armies spread over the 2-3 most threatened borders, end turn. Two timeouts in a row mark the player away (`turnClock`).
- Ending: `Conquest.roundsFor(seats)` rounds (Isles 20 for 2 players, 16, 14, 11, 9 for 6; Crown 22, 18, 15, 13, 11) set the length. Default `ending = "ceasefire"`: from round `maxRounds - 1` the game can stop after any turn (chance 1 / (2 x players alive) per turn) and always by the end of round `maxRounds + 1`; the view says "cease-fire any turn now" (`view.ceaseFire`). With a fixed last round the last seat won about twice its share in bot games (6 seats 10/12/13/19/14/29 per 100); cease-fire plus the quiet first round brought it to 77/72 (2 seats), 35/34/37/43 (4), 29/31/32/25/33 (5) and 16/24/25/24/26/35 (6) per 150. House rule "Fixed last round" keeps the old ending. Most territories wins, then most armies; a full tie is a draw. `state.history` (lands per seat each round) and `biggestBattle` feed the end screen.
- Table: `src/client/Renderers/Conquest.luau`. The map is drawn in code: a round land patch in the region's color under each territory (regions never overlap, nothing touches the frame edge), each region's name and bonus in open sea next to it (`labelX`/`labelY` in the map data), border lines, round markers in the owner's seat color with the army count, ringed in the region color. Tap your territory to select it: red rings are attack targets, green rings are where you can move armies. The side panel has the actions (place +1 / +3 / All, Roll, Blitz, move counter, End turn), your cards (pick 3 or trade the best set), the last battle's dice, who holds each region, and the news feed.
- Like Risk: Global Domination: a phase bar over the map (1 Place · 2 Attack · 3 Move, the current step lit); the blitz win chance (`Conquest.winChance(attackers, defenders)`, exact from the dice odds, memoized, clamped at 120 a side) on a tag over the target and in the side panel; lands you can't act on dim while placing or attacking; each new battle (`lastBattle.id`) pops its losses as -N over both lands and bounces a conquered one; Half / All buttons for moving in and moving armies; Trade set nudges when you can trade.
- Also: a Roll / Blitz card floats under the target; a 1/2/3 dice picker and "Blitz stops at" stepper; +1 / +3 / +5 / All placing; the income breakdown; "Next up" while you wait; cards show their territory (+2 when it's yours); new battle dice tumble; Final standings at the end (places by lands then armies, a stacked land-share bar per round, the biggest battle). Phones and narrow boards put the map full width on top and the panel below; pinch, the mouse wheel or the +/- buttons zoom the map and drag pans it. Table view: Half and dice choice in the pop-ups, the hold-for-trade pop-up, Final standings on the bar.
- 3D table: region names and bonuses lie on the sea next to each region (or float, `region.float`). Beach heights step over three levels by a greedy pass (`beachLevels`) so overlapping sandy tops never share a plane on either map.
- 3D table: while the attacker is still on turn, the last attack shows as a red arrow between the two lands, with each side's dice beside its land and the losses floating over them.
- Bot: hard bots choose attacks by the exact blitz win chance (62%+), and medium/hard split reinforcements 60/40 when two borders are in danger. Easy places at random and attacks at even odds or better 60% of the time. Medium picks a focus region (the one it holds most of), stacks one border next to it, attacks at 1.5x odds or better, takes one fair fight a turn to earn a card, and moves stuck armies to the most threatened border. Hard attacks at 1.25x, guards regions it holds, goes after other players' whole regions, and hunts players with 1-2 territories for their cards. Bots always blitz.
- Coins: Conquest Coins (`coin = "conquest"`) with their own shop tab in `shared/ShopCatalog.luau`. Rewards come from `MatchService.onMatchFinished` like every game.
- Tests: `tests/Conquest.spec.luau` (map is connected, setup, income, card sets, dice, occupy, blitz, fortify paths, forced trades, knock-outs, round limit, the turn clock, hidden hands, and bot-only games at every level that check no army or card is lost). Bot-only games take 190-490 moves. Balance (after the first Studio playtest, where one medium bot snowballed to 83 armies by round 9): low bonuses and set values plus the rally keep it close. With the conquest cap, 20-game bot simulations reach the round limit in all 3-6 seat games at every level except a few 3-seat ones (2 of 20), and in 13-17 of 20 two-seat games. The leader holds about 6-12 lands after round 3 (an even share).
- Not yet: 3D table and army models (`Assets.Conquest`), dice and army cosmetics drawn in game, a third map, 2-player neutral armies, teams. Bigger region bonuses (2/3/3/2/2/2) were tried in bot games and made 2-seat games lopsided (94/55), so they stay as they are.

# Word Tiles

Back to [[Home]].

A crossword tile game for 2-4 seats in the Scrabble family, with our own name, premium-square layout and colors. The display name is set only in `shared/GameCatalog.luau`.

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/WordTiles.luau`. Lay 1-7 tiles from your rack in one row or column, with no gaps, joined to the tiles already down (the first word covers the center star). Every word made must be in the word list. Or swap tiles (only while 7+ are in the bag), or pass.
- Boards: `quick` (11x11, 60 tiles with three U's, about 15 minutes) by default, `classic` (15x15, 100 tiles) through the Board house rule. The quick board has one triple-word square in the middle of each edge, so no two share a line (no 9x words); the classic board's triple words are 8 apart along each edge.
- Scoring: letter values (the usual English set) times letter premiums under new tiles, times word premiums under new tiles, for the main word and every crossing word. Using all 7 tiles adds 50. Blanks score 0 and show their letter in blue.
- Game end: someone plays their last tile with the bag empty (they gain what's left on the other racks, the others lose it), or six turns in a row (two full rounds with 4 players) score nothing. A game still at 0-0 keeps going (three times as long) before it ends that way. Top score wins; a tie goes to whoever was ahead before the leftover tiles were counted (Hasbro rule), and only a full tie is a draw. A person whose turn timer runs out passes (`chooseAfkMove`).
- Hidden info: `getView` sends only your own rack; others get tile counts. The bag is never sent.
- Word list: `src/server/Data/WordList/`, server only. It's the public-domain ENABLE list (about 168,000 words of 2-15 letters) plus the newer words players expect that ENABLE lacks (QI, ZA, KI, OK, EW and a few more, the `EXTRA` list in the script) minus offensive words, built by `node tools/build-word-list.mjs enable1.txt`. The blocklist is `tools/word-blocklist.rot13.txt` (ROT13 so it doesn't read as a list of slurs; `contains` drops any word with it inside, `family` drops the word and its plain endings, `word` drops exactly the words listed, and `keep` puts back clean words a rule would catch, like SPICES or BASEMENT). `tests/WordTiles.spec.luau` checks clean words stay and slurs stay out. To change the list, edit the blocklist and run the script again. `server/Core/Dictionary.luau` turns it into a `shared/WordTrie.luau` (about 0.4 s, done at server start) and `Main.server.luau` hands it to the rules with `WordTiles.useDictionary`.
- Text filtering: players can only make words from that list, so played words are shown as they are, without TextService. Keep the blocklist tight.
- Screen: `src/client/Renderers/WordTiles.luau`. Board left, rack and buttons (Play, Recall, Shuffle, Swap, Pass) at the bottom, side panel with the bag count, a live preview of the words and points you've laid out, and recent plays. Tap a tile, then a square; tap a placed tile to take it back. Blanks open a letter picker. You can lay tiles out while others play; they go back to the rack whenever the board changes. After the game ends the buttons and preview go away.
- Like Scrabble GO / Words With Friends / Woogles: drag a rack tile onto the board (or along the rack to reorder it; a short press is still a tap), a score bubble on the board next to the word you are laying out ("+24", or a red ✗), a score bar with every player (the one on turn lit) at the top of the side panel, and the bag button opens the unseen tiles (`WordTiles.unseen(board, rows, rack)`: the full set minus the board, with upper case as blanks, and your rack).
- Live word check: as you lay tiles out, the screen sends the words they make to the server (`WordCheck` remote, `server/Services/WordCheckService.luau` + the pure `server/Core/WordCheck.luau`; seated Word Tiles players only, 8 requests per 4 s) and shows "XQZ isn't in the word list" with a red bubble before Play, like the apps. Answers are cached per word.
- Zoom: when squares get small (phones, the classic board) a Zoom button shows; it or a pinch doubles the board around your laid-out tiles, and dragging the zoomed board pans it.
- Table view: a Log button opens the side panel (scores, unseen tiles, hindsight, every play) in a window over the table. The game log keeps every play.
- 3D table: the last word played glows gold with its score floating over it. Playing all 7 tiles gets a call-out on every screen.
- Bot: finds every play with the anchor-square method (Appel & Jacobson) over the trie, using cross-check masks for crossing words. Easy picks a random lower-scoring play and prefers short words; medium plays words of up to 6 letters, never all 7 tiles, picked from the top 8-30% of its options (a casual-player pace, roughly 120-190 points a game); hard adds a rack-leave bonus (keep blanks and an S, avoid duplicates and a Q without a U), swaps a bad rack instead of taking a few points, and with the bag empty values going out (worth twice the others' leftover tiles). With nothing to play it swaps its clunky tiles. Once the play store is full, the search still keeps both the best play by score + leave and the top-scoring play, so hindsight never misses the top play. Search is capped per turn; a turn takes under 0.2 s in the test runner.
- Tests: `tests/WordTiles.spec.luau` (placement rules, scoring, blanks, the 50 bonus, swaps, both endings, every bot candidate matches the referee's score, the word list loads without blocked words, and full bot games at every level on both boards that never lose a tile).
- Not yet: tile and board cosmetics (shop items exist), word definitions, a common-words vocabulary for the easy and medium bots, a daily word puzzle.
- End of game: a `{ kind = "end", reason = "out" | "stuck", adjust = { [seat] = points } }` entry closes the log, so the log shows the final sums ("Ana went out: Ana +12, Bob −12 (tiles left)").

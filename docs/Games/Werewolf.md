# Werewolf

Back to [[Home]] · see [[Adding a game]].

The classic hidden-role party game (the folk game also called Mafia) for 5-8 seats. Bots fill empty seats. Best with voice or party chat, but every seat can also "talk" with structured statements, so bots and quiet players take part.

- Rules: `src/shared/Games/Werewolf.luau`. Roles: Werewolf (1 with 5-6 seats, 2 with 7-8), Seer, Guardian (house rule), Villagers.
- Phases, all on the game's own clock (`getTimer`): roles 10 s, night 30 s, witch 8 s (only with a Witch), dawn 7 s, day (talk) 30/60/90 s, vote 30 s, verdict 7 s, last words 20 s. Night, day and vote end early once every living seat has acted, but with 2+ humans the day runs at least 20 s (`MIN_TALK`, timed by the server's phase clock via `elapsedField`). After day 8 with no winner the wolves win, so a game can't stall.
- Night: every living seat picks someone (Villagers pick a "hunch" that does nothing). That keeps `activeSeats`, the seat tags and the "x of y picked" count identical for every role, so nothing on the network gives a power role away. Bot think times are the same for every role too. Night moves have no table gesture (`TableActions`).
- Wolves' pick: most wolf votes, a tie goes to the lowest-numbered wolf's pick. If no wolf picks in time, a random villager is caught anyway. The Guardian can't shield the same seat two nights running (they can shield themselves).
- Day: one statement per seat: `accuse`, `claim` (Seer claim, `wolf = true/false`; anyone may claim, wolves too) or `pass`. Statements and votes are public.
- Vote: most votes is out, unless there's a tie or skips are at least as many as that seat's votes.
- Secrets: `getView` gives a seat only its role, its pack (wolves), its checks (Seer) and its last shield (Guardian). `roles[seat]` is `false` until that seat is out (with the reveal rule) or the game ends (`allRoles`). Spectators and the table screen get none of it.
- House rules: Talk time, Werewolves (auto/1/2), Show roles when out, Guardian, Hunter, Witch, Quiet first night, Seer.
- Bots (`chooseBotMove`): a suspicion score per seat from public talk (Seer claims weigh 3, accusations 0.6), votes against seats later shown innocent, and lies exposed by reveals, plus noise (easy 3, medium 1.4, hard 0.6). Seer bots check the most suspicious seat and share wolves they find (easy only half the time). Wolf bots follow the pack's pick, go for Seer claimers, blend in by accusing the village's top suspect, and hard wolves counter-claim or fake a Seer claim. Hard Guardians shield Seer claimers. Bot-only games split about 50/50 between village and wolves (`npm run bot-audit Werewolf`); a hard village beats the wolves more often than an easy one (test).
- Screen: `src/client/Renderers/Werewolf.luau`. A header with the phase, a timer bar and your role chip; the village as a grid of player cards (3 or 4 columns) with today's statement, votes, and your private marks; the action strip at the bottom. Tap a card, then the button. A role reveal card shows at the start. Table view (`setTableMode`) has no panel: tap a player's lantern (legal picks glow; labels over the lanterns show your pick, checks, pack, votes and today's statements), the phase's buttons sit in a `TablePrompt` bar, the role card pops up as an `ask` card, and "Village" / "Log" open sheets. If the clock runs out while a player is selected at night or in the vote, that pick is sent.
- Table: `src/server/Tables/Boards/Werewolf.luau`. Cottages round a campfire under the four-sided screen (night blue / day amber, the open vote as dots per seat). A lantern per seat (tap spot `seat:N`) glows while that player is in, goes dark when they're out, and their role card is laid face up when shown. During the vote each lantern shows its vote count.
- Sounds: `night`, `dawn` and `caught` in `shared/Sounds.luau`, played by the renderer on phase changes.
- Stats: wins as a werewolf, wolves found as Seer, players saved (Guardian or Witch), players taken down as the Hunter, games survived. Badge "Pack Leader" (5 wolf wins).
- Tests: `tests/Werewolf.spec.luau`.
- Last words: a seat voted out gets a 20 s `lastWords` phase to type one line (`textField = "text"`, filtered by MatchService into `shown`). Players who may not chat with the speaker (Roblox chat settings) see "(hidden by chat settings)" (`hideText`), and the table screen never shows players' words. Bots say a canned line.
- Hunter (house rule, from 6 players by default): when out, gets a 15 s `hunter` phase to `shoot` someone (0 = no shot). Witch (from 7): after the night picks comes a fixed 8 s `witch` step (always the same length, nobody shows as moving: she moves out of turn via `getInterruptSeats`). She sees the wolves' victim (`view.witchSees`) and may `potion` heal them and/or poison another player, one heal and one poison per game. On a quiet first night there is no victim to see or save.
- Village log: the view sends `nights` (each dawn), `history` (statements) and `voteHistory`; the renderer's "Log" sheet groups them by day with who went out and how.
- Dead chat: `getChatGroup` puts players who are out in the "out" group; party chat only delivers "out" messages to other "out" players (`ChatView.shouldDeliver`).
- Pack chat: at night, living wolves with a living packmate are in the "wolves" group, so their chat reaches only the pack (they still read everyone else). A lone wolf keeps public chat.
- Wolf count: the "2" house rule is capped at a third of the seats (`maxWolves`), so 2 wolves need 6+ players.
